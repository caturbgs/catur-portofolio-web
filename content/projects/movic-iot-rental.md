## Context

MOVIC was a car-rental product within Astra Digital International. Its workflows combined conventional booking software with integrations related to connected vehicles and field operations.

## My Contribution

I developed application and integration features as part of a broader delivery team. The work required coordinating API behavior with operational needs and validating that software assumptions held up during field implementation.

## Engineering Approach

The project reinforced the importance of treating integrations as product boundaries: defining predictable contracts, handling incomplete external data, and keeping failures understandable for both users and operators.
