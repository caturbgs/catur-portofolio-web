## Context

Xurya develops and operates rooftop solar solutions in Indonesia. The software platform supports several parts of that work, including customer journeys, operational processes, integrations, and reporting.

## My Contribution

I work across product and engineering teams to turn operational requirements into maintainable software. My responsibilities include frontend architecture, feature design, system optimization, technical research, and improvements to the way the team builds and ships software.

## Engineering Approach

The platform spans browser applications, APIs, asynchronous messaging, data pipelines, and cloud infrastructure. I focus on clear boundaries between these systems, observable failure modes, and incremental changes that teams can safely deliver while the product continues to evolve.
